Step 1: Prepare Your Computers for Sharing

Go to Settings > Network & internet > Properties (or Ethernet/Wi-Fi).

Under "Network profile type," select Private

Open Control Panel > Network and Sharing Center > Change advanced sharing settings .

For your "Private" profile, select:

Turn on network discovery

Turn on file and printer sharing .

Click Save changes.

Locate the Folder: Using File Explorer, navigate to the folder you wish to share .

Open Sharing Properties: Right-click the folder and select Properties .

Go to the Sharing Tab: In the properties window, click on the Sharing tab .

Start the Sharing Wizard: Click the Share... button. This opens the file sharing window .

Choose People to Share With:

Click the dropdown menu or text field and type the name of the person you want to share with.

For simplicity on a home network, you can type "Everyone" to give access to all users on the network .

Click the Add button

Next to the name you just added, you'll see a "Permission Level" column.

Click on the permission level to change it:

Read: Users can open and view files, but cannot modify or delete them .

Read/Write: Users can open, view, change, and delete files .

Finish Sharing:

Click the Share button at the bottom of the window .

You will see a confirmation screen with the network path to your folder (e.g., \\YourComputerName\FolderName). You can copy this path to send to others.

Click Done .

Go to Control Panel > Network and Sharing Center > Change advanced sharing settings.

Under "All Networks," scroll down to "Password protected sharing" and select Turn off password protected sharing
